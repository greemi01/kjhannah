Visual Tucker
Seashell Books. USA.
2026. Forthcoming.

We have developed a three-part means of grading Creation; “animal,” “vegetable,” or “mineral.” Many religions, furthermore, accord agency to these classes with “human,” a subset of “animal,” being the highest, and mineral, e.g., stones, being the lowest. Additional spiritual sources mention that early Creation involved the separation of “the waters above” from the “the waters below.”

No matter the heuristic that we employ to appreciate the physical universe, we are heirs to many wonders. We’re able to witness Glory as it is manifest in living creatures capable of moving and feeling, as it is manifest in plants, and as it is manifest in inorganic matter. Plus, we’re able to behold the grandeur of the sky and of our world’s waters. Our spirits are fed by these provisions. It is good to give thanks for this sustenance, both for the ability to comprehend such eternal nourishments and for their existence. Visual Tucker, thus, is a billet-doux, an expressed gratitude for these essentials.
